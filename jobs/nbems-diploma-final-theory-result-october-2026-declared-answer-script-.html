NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Result October 2026 Declared: Answer Script Request 25 नवंबर तक

National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS), Department of Examinations

विभागNational Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS), Department of Examinations
परिणाम की तारीख19 November 2026
परिणाम की स्थितिTheory result declared. Scorecard with paper-wise marks and grand total will be available shortly through candidate login. Failed candidates may request digital answer-script copies by 25-11-2026 at ₹100 + 18% GST per paper. Re-evaluation is limited to unassessed answers only; request deadline 28-11-2026 at ₹500 per paper. Practical exam schedule is tentative; final schedule will be on admit card.
