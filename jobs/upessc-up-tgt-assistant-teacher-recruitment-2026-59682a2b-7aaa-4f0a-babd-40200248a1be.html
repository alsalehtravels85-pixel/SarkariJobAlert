UPESSC UP TGT Assistant Teacher Recruitment 2026 – 17,735 Posts Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj has announced Advertisement No. 07/2026 for Assistant Teacher (Graduate Pay Scale / TGT) recruitment in recognised aided secondary schools of Uttar Pradesh. Recruitment Details - Department: Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission - Post Name: Assistant Teacher (TGT) - Advertisement Number: 07/2026 - Total Vacancies: 17,735 - Application Mode: Online - Notification Date: 23 October 2026 - Application Start Date: 24 October 2026 - Last Date for Fee Payment: 23 November 2026 - Correction Last Date: 26 November 2026 - Examination Date: 23–24 December 2026 - Pay Scale: ₹44,900–₹1,42,400 (Level 7, reported) Educational Qualification Candidates must possess the prescribed graduation/post-graduation qualification in the relevant subject along with B.Ed. or applicable teacher-training qualification and applicable TET qualification. Subject-wise eligibility should be checked in the official advertisement. Important Eligibility Update – 04 November 2026 UPESSC issued an important clarification for Advertisement No. 07/2026. As per the notice, the condition of minimum percentage of marks at graduation level will not apply to those candidates who had taken admission in Graduation in Education, Graduation in Primary Education or an equivalent course before 29 July 2011, subject to the conditions mentioned in the notice. Notice details: - Letter No.: 3295/41(2026)/अधियाचन/2026-27 - Notice Date: 04 November 2026 Candidates should read the clarification carefully before applying. Age Limit Reported minimum age is 21 years and maximum age is 62 years. Age calculation date and relaxation must be confirmed from the detailed official advertisement. Selection Process - Written Examination - Document Verification How to Apply 1. Visit the UPESSC official website. 2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR). 3. Open the UP TGT Advertisement No. 07/2026 application. 4. Fill in the required personal and educational details. 5. Upload the required documents. 6. Pay the applicable application fee. 7. Submit the application and save the final printout.
