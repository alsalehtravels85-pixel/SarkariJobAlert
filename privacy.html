PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Sarkari Job Alert (https://sarkari-jobalert.com) is an independent information website that shares government recruitment updates, admit card information, examination results and educational guidance. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit or use this website.

1. Information we collect

You can browse public pages without creating an account. Our hosting, security and database providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs and timestamps to deliver and protect the website. If you use browser push notifications and choose to allow them, a device/browser push token and subscription details may be stored so that notifications can be delivered. The website also uses browser local storage to remember certain display information such as recently loaded ticker updates.

The administrator uses a protected sign-in system to manage website content. Visitors do not have a public account-registration facility. Do not send passwords, Aadhaar numbers, bank details or other sensitive personal information to us.

2. Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and Google policies. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons/IP addresses as a result of ad serving on this website.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps. You can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center and manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features or advertising choices.

3. Third-party services and links

The website relies on service providers for hosting, database/authentication, push notifications, translation and advertising. These providers process information under their own privacy terms. Job notices link to official government or recruiting organisation websites; education-news entries may link to their original publishers. When you open an external link, that website's privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control third-party websites.

4. How information is used

Technical and service information may be used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website, maintain administrator access, deliver notifications you requested, understand advertising performance and comply with legal obligations. We do not ask visitors to submit sensitive identity or financial information.

5. Data sharing and retention

Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate this website, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the website and its users. Retention periods depend on the provider, security needs and applicable legal requirements. Push notifications can be disabled through your browser/device settings.

6. Children's privacy

This website is intended as a general information resource. It does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us to request assistance.

7. Your choices

You may manage browser cookies, notification permissions and Google ad-personalisation settings using the controls provided by your browser, device and Google. Some choices may limit website functionality.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
