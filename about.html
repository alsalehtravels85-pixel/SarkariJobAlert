About Sarkari Job Alert

Sarkari Job Alert is an independent public-information website created to make government recruitment and examination updates easier to find. We organise vacancy notifications, admit card updates, results and student-focused educational guidance in one place.

What readers can find

  • Recruitment notices with department, eligibility, important dates, fees and selection details when these are available in the official notice.
  • Admit card and examination updates with official download links.
  • Result announcements with official result and merit-list links where provided.
  • School and examination preparation guides for students.

Our editorial approach

We aim to identify the responsible department or board and link readers to its official notification or result page. Important dates, eligibility, fees and application instructions can change; readers should always check the latest official notice before applying or making a decision. Where a detail cannot be verified, it should be marked as unavailable or referred to the official notice rather than guessed.

Some updates may be collected with the help of automated monitoring. Automation does not replace editorial verification. We work to correct outdated or inaccurate information when it is brought to our attention.

Independent website

Sarkari Job Alert is not a government website, recruitment agency or government department. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any government authority, public service commission, examination board or recruiting organisation. Official department names and logos, where referenced, belong to their respective owners.
